Delete Your Account

How to delete your Landarover account and data.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

  1. Open the Landarover app and sign in
  2. Go to Account Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you:

Email support@landarover.com

We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Name, profile photo, bio, address, saved searches/listingsDeleted or anonymized immediately
Phone number, linked sign-in accounts, push notification tokensDeleted immediately
Government ID / selfie verification documentsDeleted
Messages you sentAnonymized (shown as "Deleted user")
Payment, subscription, wallet, and booking/dispute recordsRetained for 6 years as required for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, then deleted

Your login is disabled immediately once deletion is requested. Retained financial records are kept only as required by law and are not used for any other purpose.
